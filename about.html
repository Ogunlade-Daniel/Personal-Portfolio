MY STORY

Learning how the whole web fits together.

My path into development began with a simple curiosity about how websites work behind the screen. That curiosity grew into a hands-on commitment to learning how to build them.

In 2025, I completed an intensive five-month Fullstack Web Development program at UpTech Computer Training Academy. I started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then moved into server-side development, REST APIs, and databases with Python, Django, Node.js, and MySQL.

Since then, I’ve been putting that foundation into practice through projects like TastecraftMe, Marvellan Hotel, and this portfolio. I like clean, readable code, useful interfaces, and learning the reasoning behind a solution — not just making it run.

I’m currently open to full-time roles, internships, and freelance opportunities where I can contribute, keep learning, and build products that solve real problems.
